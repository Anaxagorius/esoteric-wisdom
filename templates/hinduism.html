Hinduism

A vast dharmic tradition rooted in the Vedas, Upanishads, and living lineages of ritual, devotion, yoga, and contemplative philosophy.

Core Sacred Foundations

Hindu thought is built across multiple streams: Vedic ritual, Upanishadic metaphysics, epic narratives, Purāṇic devotion, and schools such as Vedānta, Sāṃkhya, Yoga, and Tantra.

Esoteric Dimensions

Subtle body models, mantra science, yantra, chakra systems, and kundalinī disciplines form a rich inner technology aimed at liberation (mokṣa) and union with the Absolute.

Living Practice

Practice includes puja (devotional ritual), meditation, pilgrimage, scripture recitation, ethical discipline (dharma), and paths of devotion, knowledge, action, and contemplation.
