Judaism (Original Jewish Religion)

The covenantal tradition of Israel, rooted in Torah, prophetic revelation, temple memory, and enduring communal law and practice.

Foundational Sources

Judaism is grounded in Torah and the Tanakh, with rabbinic interpretation preserved through Mishnah and Talmud. These texts shape law, ritual, theology, and communal identity.

Mystical and Esoteric Currents

Early mystical streams include Merkabah and Hekhalot traditions, later flowering into Kabbalah, where creation, emanation, and divine names are contemplated as pathways to sacred understanding.

Living Continuity

Jewish religious life remains active through prayer, Sabbath observance, festivals, study, ethical responsibility, and memory of covenantal history across generations and lands.
