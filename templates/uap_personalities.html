👤 UFO Personalities

Biographies and deep dives into the researchers, witnesses, whistleblowers, contactees, investigators, and cultural figures who have shaped our understanding of the UAP/UFO phenomenon — from the earliest modern sightings to the current congressional disclosure era.

🛸 Contactees & Early Witnesses

🌌 George Adamski

Polish-American contactee who claimed face-to-face meetings with Venusian "Space Brothers" in the California desert.

✨ Orfeo Angelucci

Factory worker and mystic who reported profound transformative contact experiences near Lockheed's Burbank plant.

✈️ Kenneth Arnold

Businessman pilot whose 1947 Mt. Rainier sighting coined the term "flying saucer" and launched the modern UFO era.

🇨🇭 Billy Meier

Swiss one-armed farmer claiming decades of contact with Pleiadian/Plejaren beings, producing extensive photographic evidence.

🌲 Travis Walton

Arizona logger who vanished for five days in 1975 after his crew witnessed him struck by a beam from a hovering craft.

📖 Whitley Strieber

Horror novelist turned reluctant abductee whose memoir Communion introduced the grey alien image to mainstream culture.

🌅 Chris Bledsoe

North Carolina businessman reporting ongoing contact experiences since 2007, subject of intelligence community interest.

🔭 Investigators & Researchers

🔬 J. Allen Hynek

Astrophysicist, Project Blue Book consultant, creator of the CE classification system, and founder of CUFOS.

🚀 Stanton Friedman

Nuclear physicist and "Flying Saucer Physicist" who pioneered the Roswell investigation and MJ-12 document research.

🎖️ Donald Keyhoe

Marine aviator and NICAP director who argued in the 1950s that the Air Force was suppressing proof of interplanetary craft.

🌦️ James E. McDonald

Senior atmospheric physicist at University of Arizona who made the scientific case for ET origin before Congress.

🌐 Jacques Vallée

French-American computer scientist and ufologist who proposed the interdimensional control system hypothesis.

📚 Richard Dolan

Historian and author of the seminal UFOs and the National Security State series, advocate of a "breakaway civilisation" thesis.

🗞️ Leslie Kean

Investigative journalist whose 2017 New York Times co-bylined exposé launched the current disclosure wave.

🦅 John Keel

Author of The Mothman Prophecies and originator of the ultraterrestrial hypothesis — UAP as native Earth intelligence.

📓 Raymond E. Fowler

MUFON director and meticulous investigator of the Betty Andreasson abduction cases and broader CE-IV phenomenon.

👫 Jim & Coral Lorenzen

Founders of APRO (Aerial Phenomena Research Organization), the first major civilian UFO research group, active 1952–1988.

🗂️ Leonard Stringfield

Pioneer of UFO crash-retrieval research who compiled seven volumes of testimony from military and intelligence insiders.

🐄 Linda Moulton Howe

Emmy-winning TV producer turned investigative journalist, renowned for animal mutilation research and government document leaks.

🎨 Budd Hopkins

Abstract expressionist painter who pioneered the systematic investigation of alien abduction using regressive hypnosis.

🏫 David M. Jacobs

Temple University historian who conducted over 1,000 hypnotic regressions and argued aliens are conducting a hybridisation programme.

🧠 John E. Mack

Pulitzer Prize–winning Harvard psychiatrist who validated abductee experiences as psychologically real and transformative.

🏛️ Erich von Däniken

Swiss author of Chariots of the Gods whose ancient astronaut hypothesis sold over 70 million books worldwide.

👽 Giorgio A. Tsoukalos

Publisher of Legendary Times and Ancient Aliens TV host who brought ancient astronaut theory to a global audience.

🎙️ Media, Culture & Disclosure Advocates

📻 Art Bell

Coast to Coast AM founder who created the night-time radio landscape where UAP disclosure stories first reached millions.

📡 George Knapp

Peabody Award–winning Las Vegas journalist who first broke the Bob Lazar story and has covered UAP for four decades.

🎬 Ross Coulthart

Australian investigative journalist whose In Plain Sight and NewsNation reports advanced the 2023 congressional disclosure hearings.

🎥 Jeremy Corbell

Filmmaker who leaked the USS Omaha and other Pentagon UAP footage and co-hosts the Weaponized podcast with Knapp.

🏛️ Stephen Bassett

America's only registered ET/disclosure lobbyist, executive director of Paradigm Research Group, organiser of the 2013 Citizen Hearing.

🎸 Tom DeLonge

Blink-182 co-founder who created To The Stars Academy and catalysed the 2017 New York Times UAP revelations.

🌿 Steven Greer

Physician and founder of the Disclosure Project who organised the 2001 National Press Club briefing of 20 government witnesses.

🎖️ Military & Government Witnesses

✈️ Kenneth Arnold

Private pilot whose 1947 sighting report launched the modern era — listed here also as the first modern military-adjacent witness.

🗂️ Edward J. Ruppelt

USAF Captain who headed Project Blue Book at its peak and coined the phrase "Unidentified Flying Object."

⭐ Philip J. Corso

Army Colonel and NSC staffer who claimed in The Day After Roswell to have seeded alien technology into US industry.

🌲 Charles Halt

USAF Colonel and deputy base commander at RAF Bentwaters who witnessed the Rendlesham Forest UAP and filed the famous Halt memo.

🚀 Robert Salas

USAF Minuteman missile launch officer who reported Malmstrom AFB nukes going offline during a 1967 UAP overflight.

🌕 Edgar Mitchell

Apollo 14 lunar module pilot, sixth man on the Moon, and outspoken advocate for ET reality who founded the Institute of Noetic Sciences.

⚓ David Fravor

USS Nimitz pilot who had the primary visual intercept of the Tic-Tac UAP in November 2004 and testified before Congress.

🛩️ Ryan Graves

F/A-18 Super Hornet pilot who observed UAP off the US East Coast for years and testified to the 2023 House UAP Subcommittee.

🌙 Betty Hill

New Hampshire social worker and civil rights activist abducted alongside her husband Barney in the first widely-publicised abduction case.

🌙 Barney Hill

Postal worker and NAACP activist whose hypnotic regression produced the first detailed description of grey alien beings.

🔐 Current Era — Disclosure & Science

🕵️ Luis Elizondo

Former DoD intelligence official and AATIP director who resigned to blow the whistle on the Pentagon's secret UAP programme.

📣 David Grusch

NRO/NGA officer and UAP Task Force representative who became the most consequential whistleblower in UAP history in 2023.

🏛️ Christopher Mellon

Former Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Intelligence who became a key insider advocate for UAP transparency.

⚛️ Hal Puthoff

Theoretical physicist who ran Stanford Research Institute's remote viewing programme and became a key TTSA scientific adviser.

🔬 Eric Davis

EarthTech/TTSA physicist who briefed the Senate on UAP crash retrievals and studied exotic propulsion physics for the AATIP programme.

🧬 Garry Nolan

Stanford immunologist who analysed UAP-injured personnel, studied anomalous materials, and publicly advocates for open scientific inquiry.

🌠 Avi Loeb

Harvard astrophysicist and Galileo Project founder who argues scientists must seriously investigate interstellar artefacts like ʻOumuamua.

🏢 Robert Bigelow

Aerospace billionaire who funded NIDS, purchased Skinwalker Ranch, and contracted the AATIP programme through BAASS.

🌊 Tim Gallaudet

Retired Navy Rear Admiral and former NOAA administrator who publicly called for serious scientific study of transmedium UAP.

🕵️ Jim Semivan

Retired CIA operations officer and TTSA co-founder who has spoken about personal paranormal experiences linked to the phenomenon.

🇬🇧 Nick Pope

Former MoD official who ran the UK Government's UFO desk 1991–1994 and has since become a leading media commentator.

⚗️ Bob Lazar

Self-described physicist who claims to have reverse-engineered alien craft propulsion at a facility he calls S-4, near Area 51.

🔎 Dylan Borland

Independent UAP researcher and data analyst known for rigorous case documentation and contributions to the online research community.

📊 Matthew Brown

UAP researcher and congressional liaison instrumental in crafting the NDAA UAP whistleblower and disclosure provisions.
