George Knapp

"The Desert Journalist" · b. 1952

Brief Biography

George Knapp is a Peabody Award–winning investigative television journalist who has worked for KLAS-TV in Las Vegas for over four decades and is widely regarded as the most credentialled and persistent UAP journalist in America. He holds a Masters degree in journalism and has won multiple Emmy Awards in addition to his Peabody. His investigative career has ranged from organised crime to government corruption to the full spectrum of UAP-related topics. He co-hosts the Weaponized podcast with filmmaker Jeremy Corbell.

Knapp's location — Las Vegas, Nevada, in immediate proximity to the Nevada Test and Training Range, Nellis Air Force Base, and the classified test facilities generically known as Area 51 — gave him both professional proximity to aerospace and defence communities and a natural beat that made UAP investigation a logical extension of legitimate local journalism. His relationship with Robert Bigelow has been central to much of his most significant work.

Breaking Bob Lazar

Knapp's most historically significant journalistic achievement was his 1989 interview of Bob Lazar, who appeared initially anonymously (as "Dennis" in a segment about Area 51 and reverse engineering) and subsequently on record with Knapp. The interview introduced the S-4 reverse-engineering narrative to the public and created the cultural phenomenon of Area 51 that persists to the present day. Knapp has remained closely involved with Lazar over three decades, defending the plausibility of his account while acknowledging its unverifiability, and working with Jeremy Corbell to produce the 2018 documentary that revived public interest.

Knapp's investigation of Lazar extended to corroborating specific elements: he obtained partial records from Los Alamos National Laboratory confirming Lazar's presence there, documented the existence of a facility at the location Lazar described, and interviewed other sources who corroborated peripheral elements of Lazar's account. While Lazar's central claims remain unverifiable, Knapp's investigative work established that Lazar is not simply fabricating a story from nothing.

Skinwalker Ranch and NIDS

Knapp was Robert Bigelow's primary journalistic partner in the NIDS investigation of Skinwalker Ranch, and co-authored Hunt for the Skinwalker (2005) with NIDS scientist Colm Kelleher. His access to the full NIDS investigation and his long relationship with Bigelow have made him the primary public chronicler of that programme and the subsequent BAASS/AATIP contracting relationship. He has maintained deep source relationships with current and former intelligence and DoD personnel throughout his career, and his continued journalism in partnership with Corbell has kept him at the centre of the disclosure story into the 2020s.
