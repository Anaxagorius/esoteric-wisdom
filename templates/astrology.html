✦ The Ancient Art of Astrology

Astrology is one of humanity's oldest symbolic languages, spanning over 4,000 years across Babylon, Egypt, Greece, and beyond. Ancient astrologers observed that the cycles of celestial bodies mirrored patterns of human experience — not as rigid fate, but as archetypal poetry written in the sky.

In Hellenistic astrology, the seven traditional planets — Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn — were seen as divine messengers. Each ruled specific domains of human life, and their transits through the twelve zodiac signs created an ever-changing cosmic symphony.

The Four Elements — Fire, Earth, Air, and Water — form the foundation of astrological temperament. Each element governs three signs, reflecting fundamental modes of engaging with the world: passion, stability, thought, and feeling.

The Three Modalities — Cardinal, Fixed, and Mutable — describe how each sign initiates, sustains, or transforms energy. Together, the elements and modalities create a rich, interconnected cosmology that illuminates the full spectrum of human experience.

The Twelve Signs of the Zodiac

Aries

Mar 21 – Apr 19 · Fire · Cardinal

The Pioneer. Bold, courageous, and fiercely independent. Ruled by Mars, Aries embodies the spark of new beginnings and the warrior's spirit.

♉

Taurus

Apr 20 – May 20 · Earth · Fixed

The Sensualist. Grounded, patient, and devoted to beauty. Ruled by Venus, Taurus savors the richness of the physical world and the art of abundance.

♊

Gemini

May 21 – Jun 20 · Air · Mutable

The Communicator. Curious, witty, and endlessly adaptable. Ruled by Mercury, Gemini bridges worlds with language, ideas, and quicksilver intelligence.

♋

Cancer

Jun 21 – Jul 22 · Water · Cardinal

The Nurturer. Deeply intuitive, empathic, and protective of loved ones. Ruled by the Moon, Cancer is the keeper of memory, home, and emotional wisdom.

♌

Leo

Jul 23 – Aug 22 · Fire · Fixed

The Sovereign. Radiant, generous, and magnetically creative. Ruled by the Sun, Leo embodies the light of self-expression and the heart's royalty.

♍

Virgo

Aug 23 – Sep 22 · Earth · Mutable

The Healer. Discerning, devoted, and masterful in craft. Ruled by Mercury, Virgo seeks wholeness through service, precision, and the sacred in the everyday.

♎

Libra

Sep 23 – Oct 22 · Air · Cardinal

The Diplomat. Graceful, fair-minded, and devoted to beauty and harmony. Ruled by Venus, Libra seeks balance in all things and the art of true partnership.

♏

Scorpio

Oct 23 – Nov 21 · Water · Fixed

The Transformer. Intense, perceptive, and fearlessly drawn to depth. Ruled by Pluto and Mars, Scorpio navigates the mysteries of power, desire, and rebirth.

♐

Sagittarius

Nov 22 – Dec 21 · Fire · Mutable

The Philosopher. Adventurous, optimistic, and forever seeking meaning. Ruled by Jupiter, Sagittarius is the cosmic archer aiming for the highest truth.

♑

Capricorn

Dec 22 – Jan 19 · Earth · Cardinal

The Architect. Disciplined, ambitious, and wise beyond years. Ruled by Saturn, Capricorn builds legacies through patience, mastery, and integrity of purpose.

♒

Aquarius

Jan 20 – Feb 18 · Air · Fixed

The Visionary. Innovative, humanitarian, and delightfully eccentric. Ruled by Uranus and Saturn, Aquarius carries the lightning bolt of collective awakening.

♓

Pisces

Feb 19 – Mar 20 · Water · Mutable

The Mystic. Compassionate, imaginative, and at home in the liminal. Ruled by Neptune and Jupiter, Pisces dwells where the ocean meets the infinite.
