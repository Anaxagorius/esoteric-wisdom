Trust, Methodology, and Data Care

Methodology Notes

This page currently provides educational and approximate client-side calculations for fast exploration. Production-grade natal charts should use validated ephemeris data (for example Swiss Ephemeris), exact geocoding, timezone/DST handling, and auditable aspect engines.
