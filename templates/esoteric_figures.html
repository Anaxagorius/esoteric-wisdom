Esoteric Figures

The great sages, prophets, initiates, and occultists who received, transmitted, and transformed the hidden wisdom of the ages — from the mythic founders of the primordial tradition to the historical masters who shaped Western esotericism.

Archetypal & Mythic Figures

Legendary sages and divine-human intermediaries who stand at the mythic origins of the esoteric tradition, revered across cultures and ages as the first transmitters of sacred wisdom.

☿

Hermes Trismegistus

Thrice-Great Sage

The legendary author of the Hermetic Corpus and patron of alchemy, astrology, and sacred philosophy — a syncretic figure uniting Greek Hermes and Egyptian Thoth as the fount of primordial wisdom.
𓅝

Thoth

Lord of Divine Words

The ibis-headed Egyptian god of writing, magic, wisdom, and the moon — divine scribe, guardian of the scales of Maat, and master of all sacred knowledge from the Book of Thoth to the Emerald Tablet.
📖

Enoch / Metatron

The Translated One

The antediluvian patriarch who "walked with God" and was taken up without dying — and his angelic counterpart Metatron, the celestial scribe who stands at the throne of glory and holds the Akashic Record of all creation.
🎵

Orpheus

Divine Poet & Mystagogue

The poet-prophet of Thrace who descended to Hades and returned, founded the Orphic mysteries of reincarnation and purification, and whose divine song moves rocks, tames beasts, and opens the gates of the underworld.
👑

Melchizedek

Priest-King of Salem

The mysterious priest-king without genealogy, without beginning of days or end of life, who offered bread and wine to Abraham and represents the eternal order of initiation that stands outside time and lineage.
🔥

Zoroaster

Prophet of Ahura Mazda

The ancient Iranian prophet-mage whose revelation of the cosmic struggle between light and darkness shaped Judaism, Christianity, Islam, and the Western magical tradition, while founding the oldest living prophetic religion.

Historical Influencers

The philosophers, mystics, physicians, and occultists whose documented lives and writings shaped the Western esoteric tradition across the centuries, from ancient Greece to the 20th century.

△

Pythagoras

Master of Number

The philosopher-mathematician of Samos who founded the first Western esoteric brotherhood — the Pythagorean school — and whose doctrines of number, harmony, metempsychosis, and cosmic music became foundational to all subsequent Western esotericism.
🌊

Plotinus

Father of Neoplatonism

The founder of Neoplatonism whose Enneads describe the soul's emanation from and return to the transcendent One — a system of such philosophical precision and spiritual power that it shaped mysticism in every subsequent Western tradition.
⚡

Iamblichus

Theurgist & Philosopher

The Syrian Neoplatonist who transformed Greek philosophy into a complete system of ritual theurgy — divine working — in which sacrifices, hymns, symbols, and sacred images draw down divine power and facilitate the soul's ascent to the gods.
⚗️

Paracelsus

Physician-Magus

The stormy Swiss-German physician and alchemist who revolutionised Renaissance medicine and magic with his doctrine of signatures, his tria prima of sulphur, mercury, and salt, and his vision of nature as a living spiritual system.
📜

Marsilio Ficino

Renaissance Platonist

Head of the Platonic Academy of Florence and translator of Plato and the Hermetic Corpus, whose vision of the prisca theologia — the primordial universal wisdom — launched the Renaissance esoteric movement and shaped Western thought for centuries.
✨

Jacob Boehme

Shoemaker-Sage

The German Lutheran shoemaker whose visionary illuminations produced one of the most profound bodies of Christian theosophical writing ever created — a dialectical vision of the divine life as the interplay of light and darkness, wrath and love.
🌌

Emanuel Swedenborg

Visionary of the Spiritual World

The Swedish scientist who became the 18th century's most systematic visionary, claiming daily journeys in the spiritual world and producing detailed descriptions of heaven, hell, and the angelic life that shaped Spiritualism, Theosophy, and the New Age.
🌍

Helena Blavatsky

Mother of Theosophy

Co-founder of the Theosophical Society and author of The Secret Doctrine — the most influential esotericist of the 19th century, whose synthesis of Eastern and Western wisdom traditions shaped every subsequent development in modern Western esotericism.
🌱

Rudolf Steiner

Founder of Anthroposophy

The Austrian philosopher and esotericist who founded Anthroposophy and transformed it into practical realities: Waldorf education, biodynamic farming, Anthroposophical medicine, and a comprehensive spiritual science of human evolution.
🌙

Dion Fortune

Mistress of the Western Path

British occultist, novelist, and founder of the Society of the Inner Light — whose Mystical Qabalah, occult novels, and integration of depth psychology with Hermetic practice defined modern Western magical tradition.
⚗️

Éliphas Lévi

Father of Modern Occultism

The French ex-seminarian whose Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie launched the modern Western occult revival, linked the Tarot to the Kabbalah, defined the Astral Light, and provided the Golden Dawn with its foundational conceptual framework.
🃏

Papus

Organiser of the Occult Revival

Gérard Encausse — physician, prolific author, and founder of the Martinist Order — who revitalised French occultism at the fin de siècle, popularised the Tarot and Kabbalah, and extended Martinism across Europe and to the Russian imperial court.
