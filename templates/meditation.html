Breathe In

✦ Guided Journey

Ground yourself. Feel the weight of your body, fully supported. Let your spine be long and easy. Place one hand on your heart.

Breathe with intention. Inhale for 4 counts… hold for 4… exhale slowly for 6. Let each breath release what no longer serves you.

Visualize a golden light at the center of your chest. With each inhale, it grows brighter. With each exhale, it radiates outward — filling your entire body.

You are safe. You are held. There is nothing to do, nowhere to be. Simply rest in the vast, loving stillness that has always been here.

Return gently. Begin to deepen your breath. Wiggle your fingers. When you are ready, softly open your eyes, carrying this peace with you.
