# Hermeticism

Hermeticism is a stream of Western esoteric thought associated with the writings attributed to **Hermes Trismegistus**.  
It combines philosophical reflection, cosmology, and practical spiritual work.

## Core ideas

- **Correspondence**: reality is patterned across levels ("as above, so below").
- **Living cosmos**: nature is meaningful and symbolically intelligible.
- **Transformation**: knowledge is not only informational; it is initiatory.

## Practice orientation

Hermetic practice usually combines study, contemplation, and disciplined self-reflection.  
In modern contexts, it often intersects with ritual, alchemy, meditative prayer, and symbolic interpretation.

## Reading pathway

1. Start with the *Corpus Hermeticum* and the *Emerald Tablet*.
2. Compare late-antique Hermetic ideas with Neoplatonism and Gnosticism.
3. Trace Renaissance revival through Ficino and later esoteric movements.
